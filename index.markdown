Align Items is a small, Michigan-based business that provides consulting services focused on website design and development to small businesses and nonprofit organizations.

## Services

We provide a number of consulting, design, and development services:

- Initial Site Planning: We sit with you and map out your website needs, goals, and desires.
- Information Architecture Planning: We can help you figure out how best to present your content to your site visitors.
- CMS Consulting: We work with you to determine the best Content Management System for your website based on your needs, expectations, and budget. We can work with the following CMS options:
  - Drupal
  - WordPress
  - Squarespace
  - Shopify
  - Square (Square Up)
  - Jekyll
- Workflow Planning: We help to plan out and document any user workflows your website needs to handle.
- UI/UX Design Consulting: We can help you create a website that is intuitive and easy for your visitors to use.
- Website Development: We can take your website from proposal to reality.
- Website Maintenance: We provide long term website maintenance and support, including content changes, updates, and issue resolution.
- Website Migration Services: We can migrate your existing site from WordPress to Drupal, Drupal to WordPress

We do offer limited web copy writing, graphic design, and photo editing services.

## Accessibility-focused

Align Items is operated by a disabled programmer. We are proponents of Universal Design and do our best to create websites that are accessible and usable by all of your potential visitors. 

## Refund and Dispute Policy

We take pride in our work, and maintain a general refund and dispute policy in our standard client contract. The specific details of each client's refund and dispute policy is negotiated during the initial contract negotiation so that we can meet each client's specific needs and expectations.
